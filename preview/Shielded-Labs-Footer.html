This NU7.1 poll uses Valar. Earlier polls used Hanh’s software.
What is public?Hanh · Coin Voting / ZCVValar · Shielded Vote
During votingThe server records encrypted ballots. You can count the records. A holder can submit more than one.The portal counts accepted vote commitments (casts) per question from chain events. It also counts encrypted fragments (shares) from the round summary. Fragments can arrive later, and a wallet can make multiple commitments.
After votingWith the published election key and ballot archive, an auditor can read the amount assigned to a choice in a decryptable output and add up the results.Once the tally is finalized, each answer has a combined voting weight in ZEC. The public tally keeps individual amounts private.
Large holdersLarge individual contributions can be visible in a recount. A holder can split or delegate voting power, so those records alone cannot establish who controlled the weight.A larger fragment count can carry less ZEC. The records do not give a count of distinct voters or a ranking of holders by voting weight.
