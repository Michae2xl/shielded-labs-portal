Transparency Statement

Shielded Labs is an independent, nonprofit association established under Swiss law. Our mission is to build unstoppable private money by strengthening Zcash’s long-term security and sustainability. We are funded by donations from Zcash holders and supporters, and all funds support our mission.

We maintain financial reserves that allow us to plan for the long term and pursue our priorities independently. Continued donations help us sustain and expand that work.

We have received donations from Vitalik Buterin, Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss, the Anoma Foundation, Grayscale, and others who have chosen to remain private. We appreciate their support and respect donors’ requests for privacy.

We are grateful to our donors for supporting our mission and trusting us to put their contributions to good use. We welcome their ideas and perspectives and value their commitment to Zcash’s future. All donations are unrestricted, and decisions about their use remain under Shielded Labs’ control and the board’s oversight. Donors receive no equity, ownership, governance rights, or economic interest in Shielded Labs in exchange for their contributions. We independently determine our roadmap and priorities based on what we believe is best for Zcash.

Our Board of Directors oversees the association and the use of its funds. The board includes independent directors who are expected to exercise their own judgment and hold leadership accountable. They have a responsibility to speak up if they believe Shielded Labs is not honoring its commitments or acting consistently with its mission and values.

Some of our donors also support or invest in other Zcash-related projects or companies. These relationships are separate from their support for Shielded Labs.

If you would like to support our work, you can donate using the QR code below or email us at contact@shieldedlabs.net.
