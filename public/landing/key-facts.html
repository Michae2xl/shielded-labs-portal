About Shielded Labs

Shielded Labs is an independent, donation-funded Zcash support organization based in Switzerland. It works on Zcash protocol research and engineering, including the Network Sustainability Mechanism, Crosslink, Dynamic Fees, Zero, Ironwood and Epoch. Shielded Labs has never received funding from the Zcash Development Fund or block rewards; it is funded by donations.

You're welcome to use this paragraph as it is when describing Shielded Labs.

Projects

  • Network Sustainability Mechanism: Improve the long-term sustainability of the 21 million coin cap.
  • Crosslink: Upgrade Zcash’s long-term security by combining Proof of Work and Proof of Stake.
  • Dynamic Fees: Develop market-driven, comparable-based transaction fee mechanics.
  • Zero: Open-source Zcash infrastructure software for exchanges, mining pools and wallet providers.
  • Ironwood: A new shielded pool that restores the integrity of Zcash’s circulating supply.
  • Epoch: Develop high-assurance post-quantum cryptography for Zcash, with formal verification.
