Crosslink

Roadmap

Crosslink

A high-level roadmap for the first implementation of Crosslink

Milestone 1: Mar 2025

Placeholders and Processes

This initial milestone will provide the code structure and minimal functionality with no consensus modifications.

✅ Completed: March 21, 2025

Milestone 2: Apr-May 2025

Disconnected BFT

Introduce BFT functionality, although Zcash’s existing consensus will be completely unaltered.

✅ Completed: May 16, 2025

Milestone 3: Jun-Aug 2025

PoW Consensus

First modification of PoW consensus to respect the assured finality provided by the BFT protocol.

✅ Completed: August 28, 2025

Milestone 4: Sep-Dec 2025

Staking Transaction Semantics

Introduce new transaction functionality to enable all of the staking operations, such as delegations, PoS roster selection, and rewards issuance.

✅ Completed: January 28, 2026

Milestone 5: Jan-Feb 2026

Pre-Productionization, Tech Debt, Final Refactoring

Milestone 5 revisits the prototyping design choices to plan out the Mainnet production readiness roadmap through 2026.

✅ Completed: April 15, 2026

Q2 2026 - Q2 2027

Productionization Phase

Transition from the Prototype Phase to Productionization, which involves deploying Crosslink on a testnet, iterating on its design and implementation based on user feedback, and, if there is consensus, integrating it into the Zcash protocol.

Status: In Progress

For more detailed descriptions of each milestone, see this blog post from early 2025.
