Governance & Polling

This page provides information about upcoming Zcash polls, including both protocol governance polls used to gauge community and coinholder sentiment and coinholder-directed funding used to allocate grants.

Governance Overview

Zcash governance has evolved significantly over the years, moving from a relatively centralized model toward a broader, more decentralized process. Today, it is useful to distinguish between two related but separate functions: determining support for changes to the Zcash protocol and deciding how ecosystem funding is allocated.

  • Protocol Governance: Changes to Zcash are developed and discussed openly through the ZIP process, with community panels and coinholders helping gauge support for proposed upgrades. These are polls, not binding votes. Zcash has no onchain governance mechanism that automatically determines which changes are adopted. Instead, polling provides input into a broader rough-consensus process, with changes ultimately taking effect when network participants choose to adopt the software.
  • Funding: Zcash currently allocates 20% of block rewards to ecosystem development. Of this, 8% goes to Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) and 12% goes to the Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program, which allows ZEC holders to decide which grant proposals should receive funding. We provide information below on how to participate in the coinholder grants program.

How to Participate

Coinholders

ZEC holders can participate directly in coinholder polls using supported wallets. Vizor, ZODL, and ZKool currently support Zcash coinholder voting. Click on an icon below to download a wallet:

  • Vizorvizor.cash
  • ZODLzodl.com
  • ZKoolhhanh00.github.io/zkool2

ZCAP

The Zcash Community Advisory Panel (ZCAP) is a group of community members polled by the Zcash Foundation to provide advisory input on Zcash governance. Membership is open to people who meet one or more eligibility criteria.

  • Zcash Community Advisory Panelzfnd.org/zcash-community-advisory-panel

Other Community Panels

ZecHub and several regional Zcash communities also participate in governance polling. To learn more about these groups or get involved, visit ZecHub or inquire about regional communities on the Zcash Community Forum.
