Crosslink

Crosslink is a proposed upgrade for Zcash by Shielded Labs. Our goals are to strengthen the 21 million supply cap with the combined security of Proof of Work and Proof of Stake, and let people get paid to hold ZEC.

Current phase: productionization, tested on incentivized feature nets. See the Crosslink roadmap.

Take part

How to take part in the incentivized feature net with the desktop app, from installing it to reporting what you find. The release notes carry the details for the current version.

  1. Install and back up

    Download the build for your OS. Open the app and privately back up your test-wallet seed. Use cTAZ only. We ship weekly; only the current chain earns rewards, so upgrade within a week of each release.

  2. Sync the network

    Let the node catch up. Compare a block’s height and hash with a same-network explorer. Check that finalized height advances too.

  3. Get test funds

    Request cTAZ from the app’s faucet, or mine. Desktop CPU mining starts after sync by default.

  4. Choose a finalizer and stake

    Check recent votes, then copy a finalizer’s identity. During a staking window: Stake → Paste Identity → amount.

  5. Change your delegation

    Open Edit Stake, select a bond and retarget it to another finalizer. Retargeting also works outside staking windows.

  6. Unstake and withdraw

    Begin unbonding in Edit Stake. Once the bond is withdrawable, withdraw during an allowed window.

  7. Share what you found

    Record version, OS, time, transaction IDs and expected versus actual behavior. Recheck after restarting. Remove secrets before reporting.
